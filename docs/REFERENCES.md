# References

## [R01] Rockafellar, R. T.; Uryasev, S. (2000)

*[Optimization of conditional value-at-risk](https://www.risk.net/journal-risk/2161159/optimization-conditional-value-risk)*. Journal of Risk, 2(3), 21–41.

Expected-shortfall epigraph and scenario optimization.

## [R02] Ruszczyński, A. (2010)

*[Risk-averse dynamic programming for Markov decision processes](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-010-0393-3)*. Mathematical Programming, 125, 235–261.

Nested conditional risk and dynamic programming, with the correction in R03.

## [R03] Ruszczyński, A. (2014)

*[Erratum to: Risk-averse dynamic programming for Markov decision processes](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-014-0783-z)*. Mathematical Programming, 145, 601–604.

Correction to the original general-state result; finite trees avoid measurable-selection issues.

## [R04] Iyengar, G. N. (2005)

*[Robust Dynamic Programming](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.1040.0129)*. Mathematics of Operations Research, 30(2), 257–280.

Rectangular transition ambiguity and robust recursion.

## [R05] Gallego, G.; van Ryzin, G. (1994)

*[Optimal Dynamic Pricing of Inventories with Stochastic Demand over Finite Horizons](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.40.8.999)*. Management Science, 40(8), 999–1020.

Price-dependent arrivals and the opportunity cost of perishable inventory.

## [R06] Carmona, R.; Coulon, M.; Schwarz, D. (2013)

*[Electricity price modeling and asset valuation: a multi-fuel structural approach](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11579-012-0091-4)*. Mathematics and Financial Economics, 7(2), 167–202.

Structural interaction among output prices, demand and input costs. [Author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.2299).

## [R07] Callegaro, G.; Campi, L.; Giusto, V.; Vargiolu, T. (2016)

*[Utility indifference pricing and hedging for structured contracts in energy markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.7725)*. Author manuscript, arXiv:1407.7725, revised 20 February 2016.

Portfolio-dependent prices and exercise/hedge interactions in incomplete energy markets. Its exponential utility differs from this project’s mean/ES preference.

## [R08] Brigo, D.; Francischello, M.; Pallavicini, A. (2015)

*[Invariance, existence and uniqueness of solutions of nonlinear valuation PDEs and FBSDEs inclusive of credit risk, collateral and funding costs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.00686)*. Author manuscript, arXiv:1506.00686, revised 27 November 2015.

Cash-flow-based treatment of collateral, credit, treasury funding and overlapping valuation adjustments.

## [R09] Brunnermeier, M. K.; Pedersen, L. H. (2009)

*[Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhn098)*. Review of Financial Studies, 22(6), 2201–2238.

Interaction between funding constraints, margin and market liquidity.

## [R10] Kleywegt, A. J.; Shapiro, A.; Homem-de-Mello, T. (2002)

*[The Sample Average Approximation Method for Stochastic Discrete Optimization](https://doi.org/10.1137/S1052623499363220)*. SIAM Journal on Optimization, 12(2), 479–502.

Repeated sampling and optimization-error assessment. [Institutional record](https://pure.uai.cl/en/publications/the-sample-average-approximation-method-for-stochastic-discrete-o/).

## [R11] Rawlings, J. B.; Mayne, D. Q.; Diehl, M. M. (2026)

*[Model Predictive Control: Theory, Computation, and Design](https://sites.engineering.ucsb.edu/~jbraw/mpc/)*. Second edition, sixth printing; Nob Hill Publishing, author resource page.

Receding-horizon control and the distinction between a local controller and a full value function.

## [R12] MLCommons (2026)

*[MLPerf Inference: Datacenter](https://mlcommons.org/benchmarks/inference-datacenter/)*. Benchmark specification and results portal; accessed 18 September 2026.

Workload-specific quality, latency and throughput conventions for useful-work normalization.

## [R13] SciPy contributors (2026)

*[scipy.optimize.milp](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.milp.html)*. SciPy 1.17.0 reference manual; accessed 18 September 2026.

MILP status, dual bound, node count and relative-gap fields reported by SciPy.

## [R14] SciPy contributors (2026)

*[scipy.optimize.linprog, method highs](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/optimize.linprog-highs.html)*. SciPy 1.17.0 reference manual; accessed 18 September 2026.

LP tolerances, residuals and marginals used for numerical certificates.

## [R15] Heath, D.; Jarrow, R.; Morton, A. (1992)

*[Bond Pricing and the Term Structure of Interest Rates: A New Methodology for Contingent Claims Valuation](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2951677)*. Econometrica, 60(1), 77–105.

The forward curve as the primary stochastic object and drift consistency. Commodity futures martingale restrictions determine the drift in this model.

## [R16] Benth, F. E.; Krühner, P. (2014)

*[Representation of Infinite-Dimensional Forward Price Models in Commodity Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.4111)*. Communications in Mathematics and Statistics, 2, 47–106. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40304-014-0030-1).

Function-valued commodity curves, Lévy drivers, OU realizations and delivery-period operators.

## [R17] Benth, F. E.; Piccirilli, M.; Vargiolu, T. (2019)

*[Mean-reverting additive energy forward curves in a Heath–Jarrow–Morton framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.03310)*. Mathematics and Financial Economics, 13, 543–577. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11579-019-00237-x).

Shared additive multicommodity factors and the distinction between real-world dynamics and martingale pricing dynamics. The linked manuscript has the shorter title Additive energy forward curves in a Heath–Jarrow–Morton framework.

## [R18] Benth, F. E.; Koekebakker, S. (2008)

*[Stochastic modeling of financial electricity contracts](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140988307000783)*. Energy Economics, 30(3), 1116–1157. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eneco.2007.06.005).

Actual delivery periods and consistent aggregation of financial electricity contracts; transferred here to compute service strips.

## [R19] Duffie, D.; Pan, J.; Singleton, K. (2000)

*[Transform Analysis and Asset Pricing for Affine Jump-Diffusions](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7105)*. Econometrica, 68(6), 1343–1376. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-0262.00164).

Affine transform methods. The project specializes to constant-coefficient OU factors with compound-Poisson Gaussian marks and implements its transforms directly.

## [R20] Föllmer, H.; Schweizer, M. (1991)

*[Hedging of Contingent Claims under Incomplete Information](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~mschweiz/ms_publ_eng.html)*. In Davis and Elliott (eds.), Applied Stochastic Analysis, Stochastics Monographs 5, 389–414.

Orthogonal residual risk and incomplete-market hedging. The project’s covariance-weighted factor projection is a local diagnostic, distinct from a full dynamic martingale decomposition.

## [R21] Kalman, R. E. (1960)

*[A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/fluidsengineering/article/82/1/35/397706/A-New-Approach-to-Linear-Filtering-and-Prediction)*. Journal of Basic Engineering, 82(1), 35–45. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3662552).

State-space filtering and covariance recursion. Nonlinear observation linearization and jump moment matching are explicit implementation choices.

## [R22] Longstaff, F. A.; Schwartz, E. S. (2001)

*[Valuing American Options by Simulation: A Simple Least-Squares Approach](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/43n1k4jb)*. Review of Financial Studies, 14(1), 113–147. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/14.1.113).

Regression-based continuation for exercise decisions. The project adds a finite remaining-rights state and evaluates the frozen exercise rule on independent paths.

## [R23] Benth, F. E.; Krühner, P. (2015)

*[Derivatives pricing in energy markets: an infinite-dimensional approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.7943)*. SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics, 6(1), 825–869. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/15100268X).

Options and Greeks as functionals of an entire forward curve, and cross-commodity covariance.

## [R24] Aïd, R.; Campi, L.; Langrené, N.; Pham, H. (2014)

*[A Probabilistic Numerical Method for Optimal Multiple Switching Problems in High Dimension](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.8175)*. SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics, 5(1), 191–231. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/120897298).

Regression-based switching control. The linked author manuscript is titled A probabilistic numerical method for optimal multiple switching problem and application to investments in electricity generation. The project uses a finite operating grid, independent policy evaluation and a perfect-information comparison.

## [R25] Benth, F. E.; Krühner, P. (2015)

*[Approximation of forward curve models in commodity markets with arbitrage-free finite dimensional models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.05983)*. Author manuscript, arXiv:1512.05983.

Separates a curve-valued architecture from its finite-dimensional realization. The project uses an exponential loading family exactly closed under delivery-date transport.

## [R26] Feuerverger, A.; Mureika, R. A. (1977)

*[The Empirical Characteristic Function and Its Applications](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-statistics/volume-5/issue-1/The-Empirical-Characteristic-Function-and-Its-Applications/10.1214/aos/1176343742.full)*. Annals of Statistics, 5(1), 88–97. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176343742).

Empirical characteristic-function estimation. The conditional OU–Lévy moment weights, identification diagnostics and path-cluster bootstrap are specified explicitly in the project.
